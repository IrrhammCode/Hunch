## Decision

### Recommended: **S — ship BTC 60s Hunch for Agora + Privy**

**Why not P:** With ~25 days to 13 Oct 2026 and a solo builder, a multichain trading pivot spends the remaining window on breadth that Metropolis Track 01 and public bounty pages do not require. Rise In judging guidance (the platform listing Metropolis) explicitly scores **working demos, meaningful essential integration, and bounty requirement completion** ahead of complexity or logo/multichain surface. P risks technical-quality and demo scores collapsing to 1–2 if the trade path is not demo-stable — the one loop Hunch already has.

**Why not pure R:** R is correct framing but is already what Hunch *is* ("Swipe UP or DOWN. 60-second markets on Monad" / Track 01). R without S is narrative without proof; judges score evidence.

**Why S:** Continuity Track results show sponsor prizes flow to teams that **deepen a working base** with required integrations (Privy path, AUSD shape, Monad settlement) — exactly Hunch's remaining checklist. Optional: apply **R as free copy** in SUBMIT.md/video (already largely present); do not open multichain implementation.

**Killer reason:** Judges and bounty rubrics reward a complete, verifiable mobile trade loop on the required ecosystem; Hunch already has contracts/keeper/Privy/UI — option S hardens that loop by 13 Oct, while P risks converting a finished Agora+Privy story into a half-shipped multichain demo that fails requirement-completion scoring.

### Recommended option: **S**
Confidence: **High** on S > P; **High** that R is free narrative on top of S; **Medium** that official Agora bounty text (F1) could slightly favor mobile product framing — still not multichain-first.
